Critics Consensus

Four Rooms comes stocked with a ton of talent on both sides of the camera, yet only manages to add up to a particularly uneven -- and dismayingly uninspired -- anthology effort.

See more Maksim S @MaksimusMovies Nov 2 Four Rooms may have been critically panned upon release, but it has since earned a devoted cult following — and for good reason. Tim Roth anchors the film as the hapless bellhop Ted, navigating four wildly bizarre stories over a single New Year’s Eve in a Los Angeles hotel: a Halloween-themed party gone wrong, a mobster’s mysterious demands, a devilish lovers’ quarrel, and a surreal finale involving a wedding. Roth’s performance can be cringe-inducing at times — his wimpy, over-the-top portrayal occasionally grates — yet his character is the perfect through-line connecting these absurd vignettes. The all-star ensemble cast and offbeat, unpredictable stories make the film oddly memorable, earning it a place on the top shelf of cult cinema. Despite its critical “flop” label, Four Rooms is anything but forgettable: a bizarre, funny, and strangely charming anthology that refuses to gather much dust.
