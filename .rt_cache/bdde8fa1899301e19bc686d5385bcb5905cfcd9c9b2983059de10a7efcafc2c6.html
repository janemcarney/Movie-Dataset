Critics Consensus

A voyage to hell where the journey is more satisfying than the destination, Francis Ford Coppola's haunting, hallucinatory Vietnam War epic is cinema at its most audacious and visionary.

See more Ancient R @AncientRaygun 6d This epic budget busting war epic is the gold standard for which all war films are measured by. Mesmerizing performances by Charlie Sheen, Marlon Brando, Dennis Hopper, Robert Duval, a teenage Lawrence Fishburne as well as an excellent supporting cast turn this fever dream war epic into a visually stunning, emotionally engrossing expose on the horrors of war, the insanity it creates and the moral ambiguity that it creates. I've seen some reviews rating it a 3, to which I ask, what drugs have you been taking and can I have some too. It is nearly impossible not to give this film the highest of accolades.
