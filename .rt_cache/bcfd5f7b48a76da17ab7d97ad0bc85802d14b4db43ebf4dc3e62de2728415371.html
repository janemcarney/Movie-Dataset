Critics Consensus

Thought-provoking and beautifully filmed, Before Sunrise is an intelligent, unabashedly romantic look at modern love, led by marvelously natural performances from Ethan Hawke and Julie Delpy.

Vixo S @vixo_six 3d Before Sunrise remains in my memory less as a sequence of events than as the feeling of being completely heard by another person. Richard Linklater builds the romance through patient movement, vulnerable conversation and the natural chemistry between Ethan Hawke and Julie Delpy. Their discussions about love, death and adulthood sometimes carry the certainty of youth, but that is part of the film’s honesty. Vienna becomes a temporary world where two strangers can reveal themselves without the weight of a shared past. Its beauty lies in understanding that a connection does not need to last forever to permanently change the meaning of a single night.
