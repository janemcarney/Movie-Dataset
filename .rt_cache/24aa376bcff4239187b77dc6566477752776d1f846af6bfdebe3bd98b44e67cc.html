Los diálogos se sienten tan reales y me pareció relajante See more Vixo S @vixo_six Jun 10 Before Sunset understands that time does not necessarily weaken a connection. Sometimes it concentrates everything left unsaid. Richard Linklater lets the reunion unfold with the urgency of real time, while Ethan Hawke and Julie Delpy reveal years of longing through pauses, glances and defenses that slowly collapse. What makes the film so moving is that it never treats love as permission to ignore responsibility. Its restraint leaves room for possibility without turning a profound connection into an excuse to betray other people. Jesse and Celine may be forced to reconsider the lives they have built, but following the heart should still require honesty and difficult choices. The film is romantic because it respects both the intensity of feeling and the consequences of acting on it.
